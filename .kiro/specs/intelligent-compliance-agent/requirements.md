# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Intelligent Compliance and Risk Agent is an AI-powered compliance monitoring system for financial services that analyzes business communications and transactions in real-time to detect potential regulatory violations. The system leverages Amazon Bedrock AgentCore and Nova AI models to provide explainable compliance assessments while reducing false positives by 95% and achieving 30% cost savings in compliance operations.

## Glossary

- **Compliance_Agent**: The AI-powered system that monitors and analyzes communications and transactions for regulatory violations
- **Communication_Analyzer**: The component that processes emails, chats, and other business communications using Amazon Bedrock AgentCore and Nova Pro
- **Transaction_Monitor**: The component that analyzes financial transactions for money laundering, structuring, and other suspicious activities
- **Risk_Assessment**: The output classification (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, CRITICAL) with confidence scores and regulatory citations
- **Dashboard_Interface**: The React-based web application that displays real-time compliance alerts and analytics
- **BSA**: Bank Secrecy Act - US federal law requiring financial institutions to report certain transactions
- **AML**: Anti-Money Laundering - regulations designed to prevent money laundering activities
- **CTR**: Currency Transaction Report - required for cash transactions over $10,000
- **Structuring**: The practice of breaking large transactions into smaller amounts to avoid reporting requirements

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a compliance officer, I want the system to analyze business communications in real-time, so that I can detect potential regulatory violations before they become serious issues.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a business email, chat message, or communication is received, THE Compliance_Agent SHALL analyze the content within 5 seconds using Amazon Bedrock AgentCore and Nova Pro
2. WHEN earnings manipulation language is detected, THE Communication_Analyzer SHALL flag it as HIGH or CRITICAL risk with 90% or greater confidence
3. WHEN insider trading hints appear in communications, THE Communication_Analyzer SHALL detect subtle references to non-public information and generate FINRA violation alerts
4. WHEN legitimate business communications are processed, THE Communication_Analyzer SHALL correctly identify them as LOW risk to maintain false positive rates below 5%
5. THE Communication_Analyzer SHALL provide natural language explanations with regulatory citations for all flagged communications

### Requirement 2

**User Story:** As a risk management analyst, I want the system to monitor financial transactions in real-time, so that I can identify money laundering patterns and ensure BSA compliance.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a cash transaction occurs over $10,000, THE Transaction_Monitor SHALL automatically flag for BSA reporting requirements within 2 seconds
2. WHEN multiple transactions under $10,000 occur from the same account within 24 hours, THE Transaction_Monitor SHALL detect potential structuring patterns and generate AML alerts
3. WHEN transactions involve high-risk jurisdictions, THE Transaction_Monitor SHALL trigger enhanced due diligence workflows with OFAC screening
4. WHILE processing financial transactions, THE Transaction_Monitor SHALL maintain complete audit trails for regulatory compliance
5. THE Transaction_Monitor SHALL support processing of 1,000 or more transactions per day with sub-2-second alert generation

### Requirement 3

**User Story:** As a financial institution, I want an autonomous AI agent that operates without constant human intervention, so that I can reduce compliance operational costs by 30%.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN HIGH or CRITICAL risk violations are detected, THE Compliance_Agent SHALL autonomously generate alerts, store analysis results, and trigger notification workflows
2. WHEN normal business activity is processed, THE Compliance_Agent SHALL autonomously categorize as low-risk and continue monitoring without generating unnecessary alerts
3. WHILE analyzing compliance data, THE Compliance_Agent SHALL use reasoning LLMs to make complex decisions about risk levels and violation types
4. WHEN external regulatory APIs are available, THE Compliance_Agent SHALL autonomously integrate and query for up-to-date compliance information
5. THE Compliance_Agent SHALL operate with 99.9% uptime during business hours without manual intervention

### Requirement 4

**User Story:** As a compliance officer, I want a real-time dashboard to monitor and analyze compliance alerts, so that I can quickly respond to potential violations and track system performance.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN compliance violations occur, THE Dashboard_Interface SHALL display real-time alerts as they are generated by the Compliance_Agent
2. WHEN I input new communications for analysis, THE Dashboard_Interface SHALL provide immediate compliance analysis results within 5 seconds
3. THE Dashboard_Interface SHALL display key performance indicators including false positive rates, cost savings, and processing volumes
4. THE Dashboard_Interface SHALL load within 3 seconds and provide API responses within 1 second for standard operations
5. THE Dashboard_Interface SHALL support mobile-responsive design for compliance officers using tablets and phones

### Requirement 5

**User Story:** As a financial services organization, I want the system built on AWS serverless architecture, so that I can achieve cost-effective scalability while staying within budget constraints.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Compliance_Agent SHALL use Amazon Bedrock AgentCore for agent runtime and memory management
2. THE Compliance_Agent SHALL use Amazon Bedrock Nova Pro and Nova Micro for reasoning and analysis tasks
3. THE Compliance_Agent SHALL process 100 or more communications per hour with less than 5-second latency per message
4. THE Compliance_Agent SHALL handle 10x normal volume during peak loads without performance degradation
5. THE Compliance_Agent SHALL operate within $100 AWS credits budget during the 4-day development period

### Requirement 6

**User Story:** As a security and compliance team, I want all data encrypted and access controlled, so that I can meet regulatory requirements for data protection and privacy.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Compliance_Agent SHALL encrypt all data at rest using AES-256 encryption
2. THE Compliance_Agent SHALL encrypt all data in transit using TLS 1.3
3. THE Compliance_Agent SHALL implement IAM roles with least-privilege access principles
4. THE Compliance_Agent SHALL maintain complete CloudTrail audit logs for all API calls and data access
5. THE Compliance_Agent SHALL support GDPR compliance including right to deletion workflows

### Requirement 7

**User Story:** As a hackathon participant, I want to demonstrate the system's capabilities through specific scenarios, so that I can showcase the business value and technical excellence to judges.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN earnings manipulation text is input, THE Compliance_Agent SHALL detect it as CRITICAL risk with 90% or greater confidence and provide SEC Rule 10b-5 citation
2. WHEN structuring transaction patterns are input, THE Compliance_Agent SHALL generate AML alerts with BSA citations and 85% or greater confidence
3. THE Dashboard_Interface SHALL display measurable business impact including 95% false positive reduction and 30% cost savings
4. THE Compliance_Agent SHALL complete all demo scenarios within the 3-minute presentation timeframe
5. THE Compliance_Agent SHALL maintain public accessibility for judge evaluation throughout the competition period
